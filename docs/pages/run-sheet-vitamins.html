    The six

    What the thread says, and what to check

    #1 to #5 follow the thread's order. L-glutamine is a selfdriven.health addition. Edit "Your dose" and the run sheet updates.

    #1 in the thread

    Magnesium

    Bisglycinate (glycinate) or threonate

    Thread says
    Bisglycinate, as part of his sleep routine. No dose given.
    Reference
    Australian upper limit from supplements: 350 mg/day elemental. Magnesium from food doesn't count toward it.
    Take
    30 to 60 minutes before lights out.
    Label check
    Labels often show the compound weight. 1,000 mg of magnesium bisglycinate is about 140 mg elemental. Look for "equiv. magnesium".

    Caution. Loose stools mean the dose is too high. With kidney disease, ask your GP first. Keep it 2 hours apart from some antibiotics and osteoporosis tablets.

    #2 in the thread

    Omega-3

    Fish or algal oil, high in EPA

    Thread says
    At least 1 g of EPA a day, from a high-concentration EPA source.
    Reference
    Count EPA per serving, not the "fish oil" figure on the front. To reach 1 g EPA:
    6 × 180 mg (standard 18/12 oil)
    2 × 500 mg (EPA concentrate)
    Take
    With breakfast. Fat in the meal helps absorption and cuts fishy reflux.
    Label check
    EPA and DHA in mg per serving, and how many capsules make a serving. Third-party tested for purity and oxidation (IFOS or similar). Bin it if it smells rancid.

    Caution. On blood thinners, check with your GP. Trials link intakes above 1 g a day to a small rise in atrial fibrillation risk.

    #3 in the thread

    Vitamin D3

    Cholecalciferol

    Thread says
    5,000 to 10,000 IU a day. His bloodwork looked better on 5,000 than on 3,000 IU.
    Reference
    Australian and NZ upper level for adults: 80 µg (3,200 IU)/day. The thread's range is 125–250 µg.
    UL 80

    Within ULThread rangeYour dose
    Take
    With breakfast. It's fat-soluble.
    Label check
    D3 rather than D2. Labels use µg or IU; 1 µg = 40 IU.

    Caution. Test your 25(OH)D level before going above the upper level, and retest after about 3 months. Too much for too long raises blood calcium.

    #4 in the thread

    Alpha-GPC

    L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine

    Thread says
    600 to 900 mg, to raise choline and acetylcholine for focus.
    Reference
    A 2021 study of 12 million Korean adults aged 50+ found users had a higher 10-year stroke risk (aHR 1.43, 95% CI 1.41–1.46), rising with dose. It shows an association, not proof of cause. This sheet treats alpha-GPC as situational for that reason.
    Take
    30 minutes before a focus block or session. Skip it on rest days and keep it to the first half of the day.
    Label check
    Some products use 50% alpha-GPC powder, so "600 mg (50%)" delivers 300 mg.

    Caution. If you have stroke or heart risk factors, talk to your GP before using it.

    #5 in the thread

    Creatine

    Creatine monohydrate

    Thread says
    5 to 10 g a day, taken since he was 17 or 18, to maintain and build strength.
    Reference
    Monohydrate is the studied form. 3–5 g/day fills muscle stores in about 3 to 4 weeks without a loading phase.
    Take
    Any time. Taking it every day matters more than the hour, so it sits with breakfast here.
    Label check
    Plain creatine monohydrate. If you compete, choose a batch-tested product (HASTA or Informed Sport).

    Caution. Creatine raises blood creatinine, which can make a kidney function test look worse than it is. Tell your GP before blood tests.

    Added · not in the thread

    L-glutamine

    Free-form L-glutamine powder

    Thread says
    Not in the thread. Added here; people mostly take it for gut health and training recovery.
    Reference
    The strongest trial is in IBS with diarrhoea after a gut infection: 5 g × 3/day for 8 weeks helped 80% of 115 adults, against 6% on placebo. In healthy athletes, a 25-trial meta-analysis found no effect on performance, body composition or immune markers. Observed safe level for healthy adults: 14 g/day.
    Your dose
    Take
    Split across the day, which is closest to the trial's schedule: one dose with breakfast in the creatine glass, one at wind-down. The morning dose is optional. Prefer one dose? Take the full amount before bed and the day still counts as complete.
    Label check
    Plain, unflavoured L-glutamine. A normal diet already supplies several grams a day, and protein powders add more.

    Caution. With liver or kidney disease, only take it if your doctor agrees. If you're being treated for cancer, ask your oncologist first. Stay at or under 14 g a day.

    Source check

    Where this list comes from

    A thread posted on 26 September 2026 says Huberman named the first five when Steven Bartlett asked which supplements he would keep for life. They match what Huberman has discussed on his own podcast.

    The thread also links two products, a magnesium and a fish oil. Those are the thread's picks, not his. This sheet names no brands; buy by the label checks above.

    The doses in the thread are one person's routine. A blood test and your GP are better guides for yours.
